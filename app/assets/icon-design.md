# Student Assistant icon — Butter yellow

Selected by the user: pale butter-yellow rounded tile, ivory open book, warm
apricot bookmark and a small cream sparkle. Designed with the built-in imagegen
tool, not the API/CLI fallback. The blue artwork is an unused early draft.

The PNG is the original selected artwork. `scripts/build_icon.py` only exports
it to a Windows ICO with 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 64, 96, 128 and 256 px frames.
The packaged executable includes only `student-assistant-butter-v1.ico`.

Files: [PNG artwork](student-assistant-butter-v1.png) and
[Windows ICO](student-assistant-butter-v1.ico).

## Final prompt

Create the pale-yellow colorway of this Student Assistant Windows app icon.
Preserve the same friendly open book, bookmark, sparkle, rounded-square tile
and clean composition, but transform the entire palette to very soft BUTTER
YELLOW and ivory. The tile should be a pale warm creamy yellow like #FFEAA6,
with gentle light honey shading, never saturated orange or metallic gold.
Book pages ivory-white; book cover, binding and bookmark muted warm
apricot/light caramel accents for readable contrast. The little four-point
sparkle soft ivory with a gentle warm edge. Calm, airy, cozy, elegant, rounded
contemporary app-icon design, tasteful subtle depth, no plastic gloss. No pink,
red, purple or blue anywhere. No letters, no words, no extra objects, no
watermark. A single finished icon, straight-on, strong simple silhouette
readable when tiny. Transparent exterior with clean smooth rounded-square edge
and small even margin; no glow, dust, speckles or splash outside tile.

Reference: pink concept generated during this same user-requested design
iteration. Final PNG source remains in the generated-images library as well as
in this project.
